On the retirement of Karpe

Post30 Apr 2026 · #review #slop

I find it very sad. Some people say the duo have made the background music of their lives. I would disagree; they have indeed made my lives foreground music.

Without further ado, let's delve into the best songs of the three albums released yesterday:

  • Pasta (feat. ???): The question marks is where it really gets interesting. Great collaborations, super punchy and aggressive yet humorous tone.
  • forever alltid in love: Cezinando feature + insane Magdi verse, what more to ask for.
  • Soccer Mom 1+2: Very fun pop song.
  • 01: A well deserved 30 minutes of silence for quarter a century of being the centre of norwegian rap and pop music.

Thank you guys for reading, have a good one!
